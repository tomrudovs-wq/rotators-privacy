Rotators Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026 · Developer contact: artpiecepuzzle@gmail.com

Who we are

Rotators is a puzzle game developed and published by Tom Rudovsky (sole developer). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

No account, no sign-in

Rotators has no accounts, no login, and never asks for your name, email, or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (solved puzzles, painted toys, settings) and anonymous gameplay statistics (for example how long a puzzle took, or how smoothly the game ran) are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use Android's device backup, your progress may be included in your own Google backup like other app data; that backup belongs to your Google account and we have no access to it.

Optional diagnostics you can send

The settings menu has a "Send Data" option. It opens your device's share sheet with a file of anonymous gameplay statistics: puzzle timings and progress events, frame-rate health, feature usage, ad display events, and basic device performance class. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it, you choose who receives it, and you can inspect the file first. It contains no personal information and no identifiers that persist beyond your installation.

Advertising

Rotators shows ads through Google AdMob. There are no forced or pop-up ads: the only ads are optional rewarded ads that a free player can choose to watch to unlock additional mysteries. Owners of Rotators Complete (see below) see no ads at all.

When ads are requested, AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, device information, and ad interaction data, as described in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the AdMob data disclosure. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states, you are asked for your privacy choices through Google's consent flow before personalized ads are used, and you can change them later from the game's Settings.

Purchases

Rotators Complete is an optional one-time purchase (US$9.99, or the equivalent in your local currency shown by Google Play) that unlocks every mystery and future room, opens Master mode, and removes all ads. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play records your ownership so the app can restore it automatically on any device signed in to the same Google account. Within the app we store only a local flag indicating that Complete is owned.

Children

Rotators is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured for a general audience through Google's tools.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device: uninstalling Rotators removes it. For advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's My Ad Center. For any other question about your data, contact us at the email above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
